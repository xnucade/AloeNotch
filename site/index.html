Free, without the asterisk.

No trial, no licence key, no Pro tier, no account, and nothing inside the app asking you to upgrade. It's MIT licensed and the whole thing is on GitHub, so you can read exactly what it does before you run it.

What it doesn't do.

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no account. Nothing about how you use it leaves your Mac.
  • Only the requests you'd expect. Weather, a daily update check, and lyrics if you turn them on — each one optional. The full list.
  • Clipboard history never touches the disk. It lives in memory and clears when the app quits.

First launch on macOS

AloeNotch is an independent app, so macOS will ask before the first launch: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click “Open Anyway”. You only do this once.

Intel Mac, or macOS 15?

AloeNotch needs Apple Silicon and macOS 26. 0.6.0 is the final build for Intel Macs and macOS 15 — it still works, but it won’t receive new features or fixes.

Questions

My Mac doesn't have a notch. Does it still work?

Yes. On a display without a notch, AloeNotch draws its own strip in the same place at the top of the screen, and everything behaves the same way. With more than one display, pick where it lives in Settings → General.

What permissions does it need?

None, to start with. Each one is asked for only when you switch on the feature that needs it: Calendar, Location for the weather, Bluetooth for headphone battery, system audio recording for the live equalizer, and Accessibility to replace the volume and brightness HUD. The shelf, clipboard, timer, media controls and the ⌃⌥N shortcut need nothing at all.

How do I update it?

AloeNotch checks once a day and tells you in the menu bar when something newer exists. Click Install to see what changed; it downloads, checks the update's signature, and reopens on the new version. Your settings and permissions carry over.
